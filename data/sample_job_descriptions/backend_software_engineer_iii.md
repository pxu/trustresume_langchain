## About the role

We're looking for a Software Engineer III to join our Fulfillment Platform
team. You'll design and operate the services that power order processing,
inventory sync, and warehouse routing at scale.

## Responsibilities

- Design, build, and operate distributed backend services (microservices,
  event-driven pipelines).
- Own service reliability: SLOs, on-call, incident response, postmortems.
- Partner with product and warehouse operations to translate requirements
  into scalable systems.
- Mentor SE I/II engineers and review designs across the team.

## Required skills

- 4+ years professional backend engineering experience.
- Strong proficiency in Python, Java, or Go.
- Experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL/MySQL) and at least one
  message queue/streaming system (Kafka, SQS, RabbitMQ).
- Hands-on experience with AWS or another major cloud provider.
- Solid understanding of REST/gRPC API design and microservice patterns.

## Preferred skills

- Experience with Kubernetes and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform).
- Background in e-commerce, logistics, or supply-chain systems.
- Experience mentoring engineers or leading small technical initiatives.

## Seniority

Mid-level (Software Engineer III / Senior Software Engineer track).
